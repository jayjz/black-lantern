## Conflicts and unavailable evidence

- **Reasonix:** S12's September 30 disclosure/CVE chronology conflicts with S13's September 29 publication and S14's precise September 29 metadata. Record both; use September 29 as the earliest verified public-record date. Do not infer that the fix was available on that date. S12 reports shipment September 30; release artifact/patch diff not independently inspected. S14 associates the CVE with S13 despite the latter's stale CVE field. Report both severity labels with their source/version; neither is proof of exploitation.
- **Vercel:** index dates are stale relative to article bodies. S08's October 24 content cannot establish its exact October 23 text or managed-runtime rollout status. S09 independently establishes the original Node.js vulnerability. No historical snapshot was obtained. The April incident's October closure is not a new October compromise.
- **PHP:** the October 23 S01 entry describes a separate residual session-upload-progress condition and a forthcoming CVE-2026-115560 advisory. Do not claim the October 21 releases eliminate every PHP RCE condition. That follow-up requires unusual serializer switching and is not evidence the OOM fix failed. Full follow-up advisory/patch was not verified by cutoff.
- **Exploitation:** S11 reports traffic; S02 reports a completed research exploit. Neither establishes a named production victim of CVE-2026-109618. A CISA additional-provider field in S03 says exploitation `none` at its October 21 assessment timestamp; it cannot override later attempt telemetry or prove absence of exploitation.
- **GitLab:** targeted searches of July/September/October security material and the official archive did not establish a GitLab service compromise in this review. This is a bounded search result, not proof no incident occurred. Reasonix is another vendor's product investigated by GitLab.
- **Hugging Face:** early S18 language and attribution are preliminary; prefer S19 for the affected-party reconstruction and S17/S20 for subsequent analysis. All are live publications. No raw private logs were acquired, and no exhaustive reconstruction or independent patch validation was performed.
- **Date integrity:** unknown revision dates remain unknown. Page menus, recommendation widgets, crawl timestamps, and relative search labels were not used as publication evidence. Later dynamic content may be embedded in older pages; conclusions cite article bodies and explicit timelines only.

## Discovery coverage and reproducibility

Queries covered the exact PHP CVEs, Vercel October PHP/Node.js bulletins and April incident, GitLab recent security research/guidance and July/September incident terms, OpenAI agent failures, and OWASP excessive agency. Discovery began with October material and followed original links into the July-August incident. This is purposive selection, not systematic or exhaustive sampling of all July-October disclosures.

To challenge a claim: find its ID in the [ledger](../incidents/01-evidence-ledger.md), open the listed source, compare the named section and dates, then record any changed wording as a new observation. For retrospective certainty, obtain a dated archive or versioned publication history before asserting that today's wording existed by the cutoff. Raw articles and exploit payloads are deliberately not copied into this repository.
